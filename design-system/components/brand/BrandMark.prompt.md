Use the wordmark in headers (14px/900, uppercase, widest tracking, white on green). The wheel glyph is the app icon and OG mark; the shield only appears on the auth sign panel.

```jsx
<BrandMark variant="wordmark" size={14} color="#fff" />
<BrandMark variant="wheel" size={160} color="#fff" />
<BrandMark variant="shield" size={80} />
```

There is no other logo — do not invent one.
